About Dr Mesut İnan

Dr Mesut İnan, also written as Dr Mesut Inan, is a Cyber Security Researcher and Senior Lecturer in Cyber Security and Artificial Intelligence. His academic and professional interests span Cyber Security, Artificial Intelligence, Information Security, Neural Cryptography, Computer Science and emerging technologies.

His work connects academic research, cybersecurity education and practical intelligent-system applications, with particular interests in secure systems, AI for cybersecurity and responsible technology adoption.
