About

Dr Mesut İnan, PhD, SFHEA is a Senior Lecturer specialising in Cyber Security and Artificial Intelligence, with academic and professional experience across Cyber Security, Artificial Intelligence, Information Security, Computer Science and emerging technologies.

Academic Profile

His academic interests include AI for Cybersecurity, Neural Cryptography, Offensive Security, Cloud Security and Secure Software Engineering.


His work combines academic research, university teaching, programme development, technical development and applied cybersecurity research.


His professional interests also include the responsible and secure adoption of emerging technologies within higher education and digital systems.
