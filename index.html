About

Dr Mesut İnan is a Cyber Security and Artificial Intelligence researcher and Senior Lecturer with academic interests spanning cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, information security, secure software engineering and emerging technologies.


His work focuses on the intersection of intelligent systems and cybersecurity, including the development of secure, responsible and technically robust approaches to emerging digital technologies.
