About

Dr Mesut İnan is a Cyber Security researcher and Senior Lecturer working across Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity and Computer Science. His academic interests include AI-driven security, neural cryptography, information security, offensive security, cloud security and secure software engineering.

His work combines academic research, applied technology development and higher education teaching, with an emphasis on responsible, secure and practical adoption of emerging technologies.
