About

Dr Mesut İnan is a Cyber Security researcher, Senior Lecturer and Senior Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (SFHEA). His academic and professional interests span Cyber Security, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, Information Security, Governance, Risk and Compliance, and emerging technologies.

Research

Research interests include the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Cyber Security, secure systems, information security, neural cryptography and emerging security technologies.

AI Security

Artificial Intelligence security, responsible AI and secure adoption of emerging AI technologies.

Cyber Security

Cyber security, information security, secure systems and security assessment.

Neural Cryptography

Research into neural approaches to cryptography and secure computational systems.

Information Security

Information security management, governance, risk, compliance and digital transformation.
