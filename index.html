About

Dr Mesut İnan, PhD, SFHEA is a Senior Lecturer specialising in Cyber Security and Artificial Intelligence, with an academic and professional background spanning cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and computer science.

His academic work brings together Artificial Intelligence, Cyber Security, Information Security and emerging technologies, with particular interests in the development of secure, intelligent and responsible computing systems.

He holds a PhD in Computer Science with a focus on Cybersecurity and Neural Cryptography from KU Leuven, Belgium. His academic background also includes postgraduate study in Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science, with a focus on autonomous systems, risk and information security.

His research interests include AI for Cybersecurity, Neural Cryptography, Offensive Security, Cloud Security and Secure Software Engineering. His work also explores the practical application of emerging technologies to security, education and complex real-world systems.

Alongside research, Dr İnan has extensive experience in higher education, academic programme development, teaching and supervision across Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity and Computer Science.
